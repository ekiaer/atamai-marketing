# Welcome to Atamai

We're excited to announce Atamai - a new platform designed to take the pain out of farm compliance for New Zealand farmers.

## The Problem We're Solving

Every farmer knows the frustration: you've already documented everything in your Farm Environment Plan, your records, your head. But when audit time comes, you're scrambling to prove compliance across NZFAP, NZFAP+, regional requirements, and more.

It shouldn't take 80+ hours to prove what you already know.

## Our Approach

Atamai uses AI to read your existing documents - starting with your FEP - and automatically maps that data to certification requirements. Within minutes, you'll see:

- What requirements you already meet
- Exactly what gaps remain
- Clear steps to close those gaps
